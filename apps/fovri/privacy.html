Fovri

Privacy Policy

Updated 20 October 2026

Your cookbook stays on your iPhone. This page explains internet imports, optional AI help, subscriptions, app analytics and your choices.

Who is responsible

Fovri is provided by Kenneth Marcel S De Geest, a self-employed developer (autónomo) based in Spain. For support or privacy requests, contact degeestkenneth@gmail.com.

Your cookbook and backups

No personal Fovri account is required. Saved recipes, source links, folders, favorites, meal plans, grocery information, drafts, notes and saved photos are stored locally on your iPhone. Fovri does not operate cloud cookbook sync. Apple device backups may include app files according to your iPhone and iCloud settings.

You choose whether and where to export a recipe or cookbook backup. Backup files can contain recipe content, plans, drafts and included photos. Keep them private and manage exported copies through their destination.

Importing from websites and videos

When you add or share a link, Fovri contacts the source website and relevant media hosts to retrieve recipe text, captions, images or available video. Those providers receive normal connection information such as an IP address and requested URL, under their own policies. Available text and video can be analysed on your device. Video files used for analysis are temporary and removed after analysis. Opening the original recipe uses the source app or your browser.

Extra import help through Supabase and OpenAI

Extra import help can be turned off in Settings and is enabled by default in the current version. When it is enabled and local importing needs help, Fovri sends relevant source text, a video identifier, language information and/or selected video frames with source context to our service hosted on Supabase. The service sends the relevant text and frames to OpenAI to extract a recipe or generate a clearly labelled AI-inspired suggestion. Frames may include people or other details visible in the source video. Do not import private or sensitive material unless you are comfortable with this processing.

Your full cookbook, personal notes and unrelated photos are not sent for this purpose. The app creates a private Supabase service session without asking for a name or email. Supabase processes a session/user identifier and authentication information; our service also records request hashes, job status, usage counts and cached results to limit abuse and avoid repeat AI requests. Hosting providers may process IP addresses, timestamps and technical service logs. App-controlled error logs use generic error categories rather than recipe text or raw AI responses.

Requests to OpenAI disable response storage. This does not mean that no provider records exist: OpenAI may retain abuse-monitoring data under its applicable policies. API data is not used for model training by default. See OpenAI’s API data controls. Turning extra import help off stops future requests from the app; it does not recall information already processed.

Subscriptions, paywalls and free imports

Apple handles billing; we do not receive payment-card details. RevenueCat supplies the paywall, verifies subscriptions and supports purchase restoration. It processes an app customer identifier, product and transaction information, purchase history, subscription status and relevant app/device information. Paywall interaction events and experiment assignments may be used to compare paywall versions and understand purchase outcomes. Recipe content is not sent to RevenueCat.

The lifetime allowance of five successful imports is kept separately from the cookbook in the iPhone’s secure Keychain storage. Deleting recipes does not reset it, and it may remain after reinstalling the app on the same iPhone. It is not a cross-device cookbook account.

App analytics

Fovri uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand whether the app is working and how its features are used. Firebase Analytics automatically records events such as first opening the app, starting a session and engaging with the app. Fovri also records a basic app-start event. Analytics may process an app-instance identifier, Apple’s identifier for the vendor (IDFV), app version, device model, operating-system version, language, approximate location derived from the IP address, timestamps and information about app interactions. We do not send your saved recipes, ingredient lists, meal plans, grocery entries, notes, photos or imported source content to Firebase Analytics.

We use aggregated analytics to measure reliability and adoption, understand which features need improvement and guide product decisions. Analytics data is not made available for personalised advertising, and Fovri does not use it to track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Google processes this information for us under its applicable Firebase and Google Analytics terms. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Permissions and advertising

Notifications are used, with iOS permission, for import completion, grocery completion and cooking timers. Choose whether to receive them in iPhone Settings. Photos or files you select are used for the action you request. Fovri does not include advertising SDKs, sell your personal information or use your cookbook for advertising. Firebase Analytics is configured for measurement rather than personalised advertising, and RevenueCat’s purchase and paywall measurement is described above.

Purposes and legal bases

We process information needed to deliver the imports, AI assistance and paid access you request to perform our agreement with you. We use limited analytics, security and operational information on our legitimate interest in understanding and improving Fovri, maintaining a reliable service, preventing abuse and resolving support requests. Consent applies where required, including applicable device permissions. Legally required records are processed to meet legal obligations. We do not make automated decisions about you with legal or similarly significant effects.

Retention and deletion

Local cookbook data remains until you remove it or delete the app. Device backups and exported copies remain under the controls of their destination. Secure allowance and service-session records may survive an app reinstall.

Import-result caches and usage counters are designed for reuse and abuse prevention, with cleanup of entries older than seven days when subsequent import requests run; this is not a guaranteed seven-day deletion deadline. Firebase Analytics information is retained according to our Google Analytics retention settings and Google’s applicable policies. Authentication records, hosting logs and backups follow the relevant provider settings. Purchase records may be retained to restore access and meet recordkeeping obligations. Support correspondence is kept as needed to resolve the request, related disputes and legal obligations.

Contact us to request deletion of service information we control. We may need an analytics, service or purchase identifier to locate it and may need to retain records required by law or for a valid purchase. Deleting a local recipe does not automatically delete earlier service caches or analytics events. We cannot remotely retrieve or erase the cookbook stored only on your iPhone.

Providers and international processing

Providers include Supabase for service hosting and authentication, OpenAI for AI import help, RevenueCat for subscriptions and paywalls, Google Firebase for app analytics, Apple for the App Store and device services, Google/Gmail for support email, and GitHub Pages for this website. Source websites process the connections you request separately.

These providers may process information outside Spain or the European Economic Area. Where required, transfers use applicable adequacy decisions or contractual safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. Contact us for information about safeguards relevant to your request.

Your rights and choices

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, erasure, portability or restriction of personal information we control, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact the email above with only the information needed for your request; do not send your full cookbook or unnecessary sensitive material. We may request proportionate identity verification and normally respond within one month, explaining any permitted extension.

You can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or your local supervisory authority. Manage local records, exports, device backups and notification permissions through their respective app or device controls.

Website, support and children

This portfolio does not use advertising trackers or website analytics. GitHub Pages may process IP addresses, requested pages, browser details and timestamps to deliver and secure the site. Support email includes the address, message and attachments you send and is processed by the email providers involved. Fovri is not directed to children under 13; contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information.

Policy changes

We update this page when practices change and show the revised date above. Material changes will be brought to users’ attention where required.
