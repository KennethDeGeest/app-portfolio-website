The app

Fovri is provided by Kenneth Marcel S De Geest, a self-employed developer (autónomo) based in Spain. For support or privacy requests, contact degeestkenneth@gmail.com.

Fovri helps you collect and edit recipes, plan meals, prepare grocery lists and follow cooking steps. Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement applies to the iOS app. Mandatory consumer rights continue to apply.

Five free imports and Fovri Pro

The first five successful recipe imports are free. Failed or incomplete imports do not use a successful-import allowance. Deleting recipes does not renew that lifetime allowance. Fovri Pro provides further imports through a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription. Existing saved recipes remain available without an active subscription; manual recipe entry and backup restoration do not use the source-import allowance.

Billing, renewal and restoration

The localized price and billing period shown in the paywall and Apple’s confirmation sheet apply. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends; renewal is charged within the 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel through your Apple Account subscription settings. Removing the app does not cancel a subscription.

Open Settings → Fovri Pro → View subscription plans to see the paywall and Restore purchases. Restore using the Apple Account associated with the subscription. Restoration checks paid access; it does not restore a deleted local cookbook. Apple handles refund requests under its policies and applicable law.

Recipe sources, AI and food safety

Only import content you are entitled to access and use. Source creators retain their rights; importing a recipe does not grant republication rights. Website and video availability can change, so importing every link is not guaranteed. Cloud assistance is subject to reasonable operational limits and availability.

Imported and AI-generated recipes can contain errors or omissions. AI-inspired suggestions may differ from the original video. Review ingredients, quantities and instructions before cooking, especially allergens, substitutions, cooking temperatures and food safety. Fovri is not medical or dietary advice.

Your records and privacy

Keep independent backups of recipes you want to preserve. Device loss, deletion or software failure can cause data loss. Our Privacy Policy explains local storage, optional AI import help and purchase processing. Features may depend on internet access, third-party source sites, Apple, RevenueCat, Supabase and OpenAI.
