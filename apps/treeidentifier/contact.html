Identification help

For a better result, photograph the subject in natural light and keep it sharp and centered. Include the whole tree or plant first, then add a clear close-up of a leaf, flower, bark, grain, or cut surface from the same discovery. Do not rely on an app result for edibility, toxicity, medical, fire, or structural-safety decisions.

Purchases and subscriptions

Privacy and deleting data
