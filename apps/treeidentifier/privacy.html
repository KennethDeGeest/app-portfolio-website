AI Plant Identifier: Tree Leaf

Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

This policy explains what information AI Plant Identifier: Tree Leaf handles, what stays on your iPhone, and what is sent to outside services only when you request identification or an AI answer.

Who provides the app

AI Plant Identifier: Tree Leaf is provided by Kenneth Marcel S De Geest, a self-employed developer based in Spain. Privacy and support questions may be sent to degeestkenneth@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Your discoveries—including photos, identification results, notes, favorites, saved AI answers, dates, collection settings, and precise map locations—are stored locally on your iPhone. The app does not operate a developer-hosted collection or cross-device account for this content.

You control whether a discovery receives a location. Location permission can be changed in iOS Settings, and individual pins or all local app data can be removed from within the app.

Photo identification

When you ask the app to identify something, the selected photo and the category you chose—such as tree, leaf, bark, wood, flower, or other plant—are securely sent through a developer-operated service to identification providers. The app currently uses Pl@ntNet and OpenAI to generate an identification suggestion and helpful context.

Only photos you deliberately submit for identification are sent. Your unrelated library photos, personal notes, precise saved map locations, and the rest of your collection are not included. Camera and photo-library permission can be denied or changed in iOS Settings.

AI questions

When you ask a follow-up question, the question and relevant discovery context—such as the suggested common name, scientific name, category, and identification guidance—are sent through the same developer-operated service to OpenAI so an answer can be generated. Your private notes, precise location, and unrelated discoveries are not sent with the question.

AI and image-identification results can be incomplete or incorrect. Do not use them as the sole basis for decisions involving edibility, toxicity, allergies, health, fire risk, structural safety, or environmental management.

Service data and providers

The app uses a limited set of service providers:

  • Supabase provides anonymous app authentication, secure server functions, and usage controls. It may process a pseudonymous user identifier, request timing, request type, and technical logs needed for security and reliability.
  • Pl@ntNet and OpenAI process the photos or questions you deliberately submit to provide identification suggestions and answers.
  • RevenueCat processes purchase receipts, entitlement status, product selection, and subscription events so paid access can be unlocked and restored.
  • Apple processes App Store purchases, billing, refunds, StoreKit transactions, maps, geocoding, and device permission controls.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics may process pseudonymous app-interaction events, device and operating-system information, diagnostics, and crash logs only when you allow optional analytics and diagnostics in the app.

Usage records are designed not to contain submitted photo files, question text, personal notes, or precise map coordinates. Temporary service processing and security logs may be retained for limited periods by the relevant provider under its own policy and legal obligations.

The app does not receive full payment-card details, does not sell personal information, and does not use your data for advertising or tracking across other companies’ apps and websites.

Maps, sharing, exports, and backups

Apple map and geocoding services are used when you choose to add or view a location. Search terms or coordinates needed for the requested map feature may be processed by Apple under its privacy terms.

Nothing is shared automatically. If you export a field sheet, backup, image, or other item through the iOS share sheet, it is passed to the app, service, or recipient you select and their privacy practices apply. Apple device or iCloud backups may also contain app data depending on your Apple settings.

Retention and deletion

Local discoveries remain on your iPhone until you delete them, clear app data, or remove the app. You can delete individual discoveries and use the deletion controls in Settings for broader removal. Because the developer cannot access content that exists only on your device, it cannot be remotely deleted for you.

Copies you exported or shared, device backups, purchase records held by Apple or RevenueCat, and limited security or diagnostic records held by service providers must be managed separately and may remain for the providers’ stated retention periods or legal obligations.

Security and international processing

The app uses iOS protections and encrypted network connections intended to protect information. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Service providers may process limited information in countries outside your own, subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly seek personal information from them. A minor should use the app only with permission and appropriate supervision from a parent or legal guardian.

Your choices and rights

You can decline or revoke camera, photo-library, and location permissions; choose not to submit an identification or AI question; disable optional analytics and diagnostics; edit or delete discoveries; remove the app; and manage subscriptions through your Apple Account settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights concerning personal information. For a privacy request, email degeestkenneth@gmail.com. Requests concerning information controlled by Apple, OpenAI, Pl@ntNet, Supabase, Firebase, or RevenueCat may also need to be directed to that provider.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
