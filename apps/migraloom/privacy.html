MigraLoom

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 16, 2026

Summary

MigraLoom is designed as a private migraine journal. No MigraLoom account is required. Migraine episodes, symptoms, pain locations, medication details, notes, check-ins, and other health information you enter remain stored on your iPhone.

Information processed by services

Apple processes App Store downloads and subscription payments. MigraLoom may use product analytics and crash diagnostics to understand general app usage and reliability. These services may process identifiers, purchase status, product interaction, and crash information. Your migraine journal content is not included in analytics events.

Exports

When you create a visit summary, MigraLoom prepares it locally and opens Apple’s sharing interface. You decide whether to share it and which service receives it. Any destination you select has its own privacy practices.

Your choices

You can review, edit, and delete entries in the app. Removing the app deletes locally stored information unless it is preserved in a device backup. Subscriptions can be managed through your Apple account.
